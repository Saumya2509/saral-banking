SARAL BANKING — 5-MINUTE JUDGE PRESENTATION & DEMO SCRIPT
Target Audience: Hackathon Judges (Fintech, Accessibility, System Design)
Total Duration: 5 Minutes (300 Seconds)
Tone: Empathetic, confident, human-centered, institutional-grade
================================================================================

[00:00 - 01:00] MINUTE 1: THE HOOK & THE HUMAN PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN ACTION:
- Start on cold launch Splash Screen (http://localhost:3000/splash).
- Let the institutional typography, security badge, and calm animation show.

SPEAKER:
"Respected Judges, digital banking in India has grown exponentially with UPI, 
but along the way, we left millions behind.

For a 68-year-old grandfather in Jammu, modern banking apps feel like an 
obstacle course—crammed with credit card loans, gaming spin-wheels, and cryptic 
transaction codes like 'UPI/DR/192837/REV'. One wrong tap, and he fears his life 
savings are gone.

For a 15-year-old high schooler, traditional apps give zero financial autonomy, 
forcing them into cash.

And for working professionals, apps are riddled with dark patterns and ads 
instead of calm financial clarity.

We asked ourselves: What if a banking app treated technology not as a billboard, 
but as a public utility?

Welcome to Saral Banking—a multi-generational, hyper-accessible digital banking 
standard built for Bharat. It adapts to who is holding the phone: Priya, 
a working professional; Shankar Prasad, an elder citizen; and Aarav, a young 
teen saver."


[01:00 - 02:15] MINUTE 2: SHANKAR PRASAD & ACCESSIBLE TAMIL READ-ALOUD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN ACTION:
1. Tap "Sign In" (/signin).
2. Show clean demo login (Demo PIN: 1234 or Biometric Fingerprint unlock).
3. Select Shankar Prasad (68 years, Simple Mode).
4. Once on Home, tap the "தமிழ்" pill on the top header.
5. Pause 2 seconds as the app speaks aloud:
   "வணக்கம் சங்கர் பிரசாத். இருப்புத் தொகை: ஒரு லட்சத்து ஐம்பதாயிரம் ரூபாய்."
6. Tap "See my money" (என் பணத்தைப் பார்க்கவும்) and bottom navigation tabs 
   to show how every button speaks its label.

SPEAKER:
"Let’s log in as Shankar Prasad, 68 years old.

Watch how the interface immediately transforms into Simple Mode. No tiny 
buttons, no hidden menus, no confusing jargon. Every button is at least 
56 pixels tall, meeting strict WCAG AAA touch targets.

Now, notice something crucial: when Shankar selects Tamil, the entire system 
doesn't just translate text—it comes alive with voice.

(Pause while Tamil voice speaks: 'வணக்கம் சங்கர் பிரசாத்...')

It reads his balance as 'ஒரு லட்சத்து ஐம்பதாயிரம் ரூபாய்'—correctly using Indian 
numbering systems (Lakhs and Crores) rather than Western millions. 

Even better: this runs 100% offline using the Web Speech API. We do not send 
a single syllable of his financial data to external cloud servers.

Every single button, tab, and card can be read aloud, giving dignity and 
independence to elderly or visually impaired citizens who shouldn't have to 
depend on someone else to check their pension."


[02:15 - 03:15] MINUTE 3: YOUNG SAVER & GUARDIAN SUPERVISION (AARAV & PRIYA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN ACTION:
1. Switch account to Aarav Mehta (15 years, Young Saver mode).
2. Show the Young Saver interface:
   - Zero loan ads, zero debt offers, no utility bill distractions.
   - Prominent Savings Goals (School books, Laptop, Tuition).
3. Tap "Send Money" or initiate a payment over ₹5,000 to trigger the Guardian 
   Approval Request to Mom (Priya Sharma).

SPEAKER:
"Now, let's step into the shoes of Aarav, 15 years old.

Under RBI guidelines, financial literacy starts early. But minors shouldn't 
be exposed to credit cards, loans, or unregulated transfers.

In Saral's Young Saver mode, the app automatically strips away all debt 
instruments and distractions, replacing them with Savings Goals.

If Aarav tries to make a payment above his daily limit, the app doesn't just 
throw a cold error—it sends a structured Guardian Approval Request straight 
to his mother, Priya.

Priya receives the request on her device, reviews the merchant name and reason, 
and approves it with one tap. This gives Aarav financial agency while keeping 
family security absolute."


[03:15 - 04:15] MINUTE 4: FRAUD DEFENSE, STRICT QR SCANNING & PRIVACY FIREWALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN ACTION:
1. Tap the QR Code Scanner (camera icon).
2. Show camera view with laser viewfinder.
3. Show how a malicious link, text QR, or Wi-Fi code is instantly rejected with 
   the calm, localized message:
   "This is not a payment QR code. Try another one."
   ("இது பணம் செலுத்தும் QR குறியீடு அல்ல. வேறொன்றை முயற்சிக்கவும்.")
4. Open Ask Saral AI (help chat).
5. Type: "My PIN is 1234, why did my transfer fail?"
6. Show client-side redaction firewall in action:
   "My PIN is [OTP/PIN HIDDEN], why did my transfer fail?"

SPEAKER:
"Now let's talk about the biggest fear in Indian digital banking today: 
scams and fraud.

First: QR Scams. Phishing QR codes at tea stalls often trick users into opening 
malicious websites or sending money without a verified UPI VPA. In Saral 
Banking, our parser strictly enforces the NPCI upi://pay standard. If someone 
scans a website URL, Wi-Fi code, or malicious QR, the scanner calmly rejects 
it before anything can happen. The app will never open an external browser link.

Second: Our AI Assistant & Scams Defense. If a distressed elder types their 
OTP or PIN into our chat, our client-side regex firewall redacts it before 
the message ever leaves their browser. The server never sees the PIN.

And if a customer suspects fraud, one tap links them directly to the National 
Cybercrime Helpline 1930 and instant card freezing."

Judges, digital banking shouldn't be stressful, loud, or exclusive. Whether it's

Safe. Simple. Human.

Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"


================================================================================
JUDGE Q&A DEFENSE CHEAT-SHEET
================================================================================
Q: "Why did you remove Bill Pay and Mobile Recharge?"
A: "Real institutional banking apps should prioritize core financial services 
   (Transfers, Accounts, Statements, Services, Security) rather than functioning 
   as e-commerce lead generators. Removing non-essential billers reduces clutter 
   and eliminates scam vectors for vulnerable customers."

Q: "Does the Tamil voice send user data to Google or third-party servers?"
A: "No. All text-to-speech runs natively on the device via the Web Speech API. 
   Financial balances and spoken labels never leave the customer's hardware."

Q: "How does the app prevent rounding errors in money calculations?"
A: "All currency calculations use integer paise (₹1 = 100 paise). Float arithmetic 
   is strictly prohibited in the codebase to prevent floating-point inaccuracies."

Q: "What happens if a user is offline and uses the AI assistant?"
A: "Our AI assistant includes an offline keyword-matching fallback engine. Even if 
   the network drops or the server is unavailable, customers still get instant 
   answers for balance checks, transaction queries, and scam guidance."
